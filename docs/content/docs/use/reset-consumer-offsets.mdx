---
title: Reset a consumer group's offsets
description: Move, delete, or clear the committed offsets of a consumer group.
---

Each change needs a cluster with `writable: true` and the [privilege](/docs/reference/privileges) the section names.

Kafka changes the offsets only of a group without members. Before you reset offsets or delete a group, stop the consumers of the group. The group page shows the members on the **Members** tab.

## Move the committed offsets

You need `reset_offsets`.

1. Open the group from **Consumer groups** and select **Reset offsets**.
2. Pick a **Topic**, or keep **Every committed topic**.
3. Optional: when you picked a topic, pick a **Partition**, or keep **All partitions**.
4. In **Reset to**, pick the target:
   - **Earliest offset** moves to the start of each partition.
   - **Latest offset** moves to the end of each partition.
   - **Offset** moves to the offset you enter.
   - **Shift by** moves by the number of records you enter. A negative number moves back.
   - **Date and time** moves to the first record at or after the time you enter.
5. Check the **Preview**. It lists each partition with the committed offset, the new offset, and the lag after the reset. klens changes nothing until you confirm.
6. Select **Reset offsets**.

The consumers continue from the new offsets when they rejoin. While the group has members, **Reset offsets** stays disabled, and the preview still works.

**Shift by** fails on a partition where the group has no committed offset. Pick a topic or a partition with committed offsets, or use another target.

## Delete the offsets of one topic

You need `delete_offsets`. Use this when a group stopped reading a topic and you want its old offsets gone.

1. Open the group, open the **⋯** menu, and select **Delete offsets**.
2. Pick the **Topic**. klens suggests a committed topic that no member consumes.
3. Select **Delete offsets**.

Kafka deletes the offsets only of a topic that no member of the group consumes. If the group reads the topic again, its consumers start from their `auto.offset.reset` policy.

## Delete a group

You need `delete_groups`.

1. Open the group, open the **⋯** menu, and select **Delete group**.
2. Type the group ID to confirm, and select **Delete group**.

Kafka deletes the group and its committed offsets. You cannot undo the deletion.
